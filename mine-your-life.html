The problem

A resume records part of a life. It doesn't set the limits of what you can do.

The things you're best at often feel ordinary to you, because you learned them by living: stretching a budget, calming a tense room, teaching yourself a tool at midnight, getting close to a world before anyone invited you in.

Personality tests hand you a generic label. Coaching is expensive and hard to find. Mine Your Life sits in between: a structured process where AI does the careful listening and pattern-finding, and you stay in charge of what's true.

How it works

Six phases, from stories to a real-world test.

Each phase is a focused conversation. You tell stories; the AI asks the follow-up questions, tracks the patterns, and labels what's proven versus what's still a hunch. You can correct anything.

What you walk away with

A report built on your own evidence.

  • Your Mine Your Life Report: an integrated picture of what you can do, not a pile of exercises.
  • Talents with receipts: each one marked strong, emerging, or still open, and traced to the stories behind it.
  • A shortlist of directions: grounded in your life, not generic career advice.
  • A first experiment: a small test you can run this month, with a clear rule for what to do next.

Your story stays yours

Built for personal material.

You share only what you're comfortable sharing, and you can use initials or general descriptions for other people. Your stories are used to build your report, not sold or shared. You decide what's saved and can ask for it to be deleted.
